Exercises


Follow the instructions in each exercise to complete them correctly.

Make sure to read and/or listen to the text to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the activity.

For each exercise there are four main buttons to use:
Clears all answers and starts the exercise again.

Shows exercise solution.

Checks all submitted answers. An X and a checkmark indicate correct and incorrect answers.

When the exercise is correct, the Next button will appear. Click on it to move to the next exercise.

In some activities, you may need to use the scroll bar on the right to see all the questions or answers.

To move to the next exercise, use the exercise bar at the bottom of the screen.
